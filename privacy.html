My Money privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026

My Money is a small personal finance app for tracking money in, expenses and plans. This page explains what it keeps about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google. This is only used to sign you in and to send sign-in codes.
  • Your money records (entries, plans, lists, settings and receipt photos). These are encrypted on your phone before they are saved, so they are stored only in a scrambled form that nobody else can read, including the person who runs the app.

Signing in with Google

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google tells My Money your name, email address and profile picture. Nothing else from your Google account is read, and My Money never posts anything or acts on your Google account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't show ads or track you across other sites.

Services we use

Accounts and encrypted records are stored with Supabase. Sign-in emails are sent through Brevo. When you choose to scan a receipt or import a bank statement, that picture or file is sent to an AI service to read it, and is not kept afterwards.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email addaiprincessnhyira@gmail.com and it will be removed.
